Privacy policy

Your voice stays yours.

Sayso turns your speech into text and actions on your own PC. Your voice is never uploaded, we don't run accounts, and we don't sell anything about you. Sayso AI sends the text of some requests, and of your dictation, to an AI service to understand and tidy them. Once a day Sayso also sends a few anonymous speed numbers, never your words; you can switch that off.

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Speech is recognised on your PC. Recordings are never uploaded, with or without Sayso AI.
  • Sayso AI sends text, never audio. The words of a request Sayso needs help with, and of each dictation (to fix its punctuation), go to our proxy and Anthropic (Claude). Never audio, never your files.
  • No account, no ads. Sayso has no sign-in. It shares anonymous performance numbers (timings and error types, never what you say or type) to get faster; switch it off any time.
  • Everything it keeps lives on your PC, where you can read it or delete it.

The Sayso app

Your voice. The microphone opens only while you hold the shortcut, or briefly after, and closes again a few seconds later. If you turn on the optional "Hey Sayso" wake word, the microphone stays on and listens for the phrase on your PC; nothing is recorded or stored until you say it. Audio is kept in memory only while it's being turned into text, then thrown away.

What stays on your PC. To help you (and us, if you send them to us) fix mistakes, Sayso keeps a small log of what it heard and did in its own logs folder: an app log and a history of transcripts. Your settings are saved in the same app folder. None of this is sent anywhere. You can open the folder from the tray menu (Open logs folder) and delete it at any time.

Your reminders, lists, notes and memories. What you ask Sayso to remind you of, add to a list, note down or remember ("remember that my sister's birthday is 3 March") is kept in one file on your PC (%APPDATA%\Sayso\assistant.json). It is never uploaded as a whole; only the few lines that matter to a question go with it (see Sayso AI below). Say "forget that", or delete the file, to remove them.

When it goes online. Only for these, and only when you ask:

  • Weather: Sayso asks Open-Meteo for the forecast. If you haven't set a city, it estimates your location from your internet address using ipwho.is.
  • Questions it can't answer itself: Sayso AI answers on the bar (see below). If that isn't reachable, it opens your chosen AI assistant (for example Claude or ChatGPT) or Google in your browser, with your question filled in. From then on, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Web and YouTube searches, and websites you ask to open, open in your browser in the same way.
  • Models: the speech and language models come inside the download. Sayso may contact Hugging Face to check them for updates, and downloads the optional AI model only if you turn that feature on. None of this includes anything you've said.
  • Updates: once a day Sayso downloads a tiny public file from this website to see if there's a new version. It sends nothing about you; our host sees your internet address, as with any page visit.

Sayso AI

Sayso AI uses Claude, made by Anthropic, for what Sayso can't do on your PC. Everyday commands like "open WhatsApp" or "volume 30", and quick answers like the time, never use it.

  • What's sent, and when: the text of a request Sayso needs help with: a command it didn't understand, a question it can't answer itself (including anything you ask after "Hey Sayso"), or text you selected and asked it to rewrite, explain or summarise. To understand commands it also sends the names of your installed and open apps. Never audio, files, screenshots or anything else on your PC.
  • What you told it to remember: when you ask a question, up to ten short things you asked Sayso to remember that share words with the question go along with it, so the answer knows you (for example your sister's birthday when you ask what to get her). Nothing else from your memories, lists or notes is sent.
  • Your calendar: if you connect it, Sayso reads it on your PC through its private iCal link, which is stored only on your PC and never sent to us. When you ask a question about your time ("do I have time for lunch before my meeting?") that Sayso can't answer itself, the titles and times of today's and tomorrow's events go along with it. Nothing else from your calendar is sent. If you sign in with Google, Sayso can also add an event to your Google Calendar when you ask it to (and take it off again if you say undo), straight from your PC to Google. Nothing else changes.
  • Smart dictation: the text of each dictation is sent so its punctuation, capitals and filler words can be fixed before it's typed. If Sayso AI can't be reached, Sayso types its own cleaned-up version instead.
  • Where it goes: to Sayso's own small server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes it to Anthropic's API and sends back the answer. The server doesn't log or keep what you said. To enforce a fair daily limit it keeps a count per day for a random ID made on your PC and for your internet address, both stored only as one-way scrambled codes.
  • Anthropic processes the text to produce the answer, under its privacy policy and commercial terms for API use. Requests aren't linked to your name or any account.
  • Our key stays ours: the app holds no API key. The key lives only on our server, so it can't be taken from the download.

Sayso also has an optional cloud speech mode that is off unless you turn it on yourself in its settings and add your own API key; if you do, your recordings are sent to that provider under its terms.

Typing for you. Sayso types by briefly placing text on your clipboard and pasting it, then puts your previous clipboard back.

Anonymous performance numbers

To make Sayso faster on everyday laptops, it keeps a few numbers about how it's doing and sends them to us once a day. It's on by default, and you can switch it off any time: untick "Share anonymous performance numbers" in Sayso's dashboard or in its tray menu (right-click the Sayso icon by the clock). Off means nothing is collected, and anything waiting to be sent is deleted.

  • What's sent: how long things took (from "Hey Sayso" to the chime, how long you spoke, how quickly Sayso noticed you'd stopped, how long recognising your speech and answering took, how long Sayso took to start, how much processor and memory it uses), the speech model it used, how many words it typed, how many of them you corrected and how many words it learned (counts only), the type of any error (like "TimeoutError", never its message), and your PC's basics: Sayso's version, Windows version, processor model, number of cores, memory, whether you're on battery, your language setting, and whether the wake word and voice replies are on.
  • What's never sent: your voice, what you said or typed, what Sayso typed, app or window names, file names, contacts, your calendar, websites, or anything else on your PC.
  • Not tied to you: the numbers carry a random ID made by Sayso, not your name, your PC's name or anything from it; switching the setting off and on again makes a new one. Our server doesn't record your internet address. Because we can't tell which ID is yours, we can't look up or delete your numbers on request, so they delete themselves: everything is kept for at most 90 days.
  • Where it goes: to Sayso's own server (the same Cloudflare Worker as Sayso AI), which keeps only the numbers on the list above in Cloudflare's analytics storage, and throws away anything else.

This website

justsayso.lol is a plain web page with no cookies, no analytics and no ad trackers. To show it to you, a few companies see your internet address, like any website visit:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces (see Google Fonts privacy).

The "Try it" demo on the home page is a simulation that runs in your browser. It never uses your microphone. The download itself is served by Cloudflare, which sees your internet address like any download.
